### Staffing: Peak vs. Off-Season

Sunset Bay Resorts runs three concession stands in Florida. Peak season runs November through April. During peak season, each stand has one lead operator and two line staff. That is 9 staff members on duty daily. Off-season runs May through October. Then each stand has one lead operator and one line staff, or 6 staff members on duty daily.

We keep a trained backup pool. These staff cover sick days, vacations, and demand surges. Each backup completes the same training as regular staff. Backups stay on call during peak season. They respond within two hours.
